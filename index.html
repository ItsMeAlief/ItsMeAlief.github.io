Technical Skills & Areas of Interest

[ 6 PILLARS ]
SEC_01 HARDWARE

Electronics & PCB Development

I am interested in electronic circuit design, PCB layout, component selection, power regulation, signal interfacing, and hardware integration. My project interests include custom controller boards, sensor interfaces, power distribution, communication systems, and compact embedded hardware.

I enjoy investigating component datasheets, evaluating electrical specifications, comparing alternative components, and understanding how design decisions affect performance, reliability, and manufacturability.

PCB Layout Power Reg Signals Datasheets
SEC_02 FIRMWARE

Embedded & Microcontrollers

My interests include microcontroller-based development, embedded C, Python programming, and the integration of hardware with firmware. I explore platforms such as STM32 and ESP32, along with communication interfaces including UART, CAN, RS-485, USB, and other industrial protocols.

I am particularly interested in building custom electronics that combine multiple interfaces, sensors, control functions, and communication capabilities into a coherent system.

STM32 ESP32 Embedded C CAN / RS-485
SEC_03 ROBOTICS

UAV & FPV Drone Engineering

I have a strong interest in multirotor aircraft, FPV systems, and custom UAV development. My technical explorations include flight controllers, electronic speed controllers, motor and propeller selection, battery systems, radio links, video transmission, and system-level integration.

I am also interested in advanced UAV architectures, custom flight-control hardware, high-current power distribution, GPS integration, and long-term improvements in flight-system reliability and capability.

Flight Ctrl ESC Tuning RF Telemetry Power Dist
SEC_04 MECHANICAL

Mechanical Design & CAD

I am interested in 3D modeling, mechanical assemblies, custom enclosures, fabrication, and the design of functional hardware. My areas of exploration include 3D printing, motion systems, CNC-related electronics, and custom mechanical platforms.

I value designs that are not only technically sound but also practical to assemble, maintain, modify, and manufacture (DFM).

3D CAD 3D Printing Enclosures DFM
SEC_05 DIAGNOSTICS

Computer Hardware & Diagnostics

I have an interest in computer architecture, laptop hardware, GPU and memory configurations, thermal and power behavior, driver-level troubleshooting, and hardware compatibility.

My approach to troubleshooting emphasizes identifying the underlying cause of a problem rather than relying solely on superficial fixes: examining system behavior, comparing configurations, and testing potential solutions systematically.

Thermals Architecture Root-Cause Telemetry

Civil & Structural Engineering

My academic background includes civil engineering fundamentals, structural analysis, construction engineering, reinforced concrete, steel structures, geotechnical engineering, hydraulics, and earthquake engineering.

How I Approach Engineering

[ 5 PRINCIPLES ]
[ 01 ]

System-Level Thinking

I look beyond individual components to understand how hardware, software, mechanical structures, and power systems interact as a complete system.

[ 02 ]

Independent Learning

I actively explore technical documentation, component datasheets, engineering standards, open-source projects, and alternative design approaches to expand my knowledge.

[ 03 ]

Analytical Troubleshooting

I prefer to break complex problems into smaller parts, investigate possible failure mechanisms, evaluate evidence, and work toward solutions based on technical reasoning.

[ 04 ]

Practical Design

I am interested in turning concepts into functional designs, whether through circuit development, CAD modeling, firmware, hardware integration, or physical prototyping.

[ 05 ]

Continuous Iteration

I see engineering as an iterative process of learning, testing, evaluating results, and refining designs to improve their functionality, reliability, and efficiency.
